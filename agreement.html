Cookie Preferences

We use cookies to enhance your experience on our website. You can choose which types of cookies to allow:

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be switched off. They are usually set in response to actions made by you which amount to a request for services, such as setting your privacy preferences, logging in, or filling in forms.

Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. We use Google Analytics to measure website performance and improve user experience.

Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization, such as remembering your preferences, language settings, and providing live chat support.

Marketing Cookies

These cookies track visitors across websites to display relevant advertisements and measure campaign effectiveness. We do not use third-party marketing cookies, but may use them for our own marketing analysis.

4. Backup and Recovery

4.1 Backup Service Levels

  • Weekly Backups: 99.5% success rate, 4-week retention
  • Nightly Backups: 99.9% success rate, 30-day retention
  • Backup Verification: Automated integrity checks on all backups
  • Cross-Region Backup: Secondary backup storage in alternate Canadian data center

4.2 Disaster Recovery

  • Recovery Time Objective (RTO): 4 hours for critical systems
  • Recovery Point Objective (RPO): 24 hours maximum data loss for weekly backups, 1 hour for nightly backups
  • Failover Testing: Quarterly disaster recovery tests
  • Geographic Redundancy: Data replicated across multiple Canadian regions

5. Security and Compliance

5.1 Security Standards

  • Data Encryption: AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit
  • Network Security: DDoS protection, intrusion detection, firewall management
  • Access Controls: Multi-factor authentication, role-based access
  • Vulnerability Management: Monthly security scans, patch management within 48 hours of critical updates

5.2 Compliance Commitments

  • PIPEDA Compliance: Full adherence to Canadian privacy legislation
  • Data Residency: 100% Canadian data storage guarantee
  • Audit Compliance: Annual third-party security audits
  • Incident Reporting: Security incidents reported within 2 hours of detection

6. Maintenance and Updates

6.1 Scheduled Maintenance

  • Maintenance Windows: Sundays 2:00 AM - 6:00 AM EST
  • Advance Notice: Minimum 72 hours notice for all maintenance
  • Emergency Maintenance: Immediate notification with detailed impact assessment
  • Maintenance Impact: Maximum 2 hours downtime per month for scheduled maintenance

6.2 Software Updates

  • Operating System Updates: Customer-controlled update scheduling
  • Security Patches: Critical patches applied within 48 hours (with customer approval)
  • Infrastructure Updates: Zero-downtime updates where possible
  • Version Support: Minimum 12 months support for all software versions

7. Service Credits and Remedies

7.1 Service Credit Calculation

Service credits are calculated based on the monthly service fee for affected services:

  • 99.0% - 99.89% uptime: 10% service credit
  • 95.0% - 98.99% uptime: 25% service credit
  • Below 95.0% uptime: 50% service credit

7.2 Service Credit Process

  • Claim Period: Service credits must be requested within 30 days of the incident
  • Credit Application: Credits applied to the following month's invoice
  • Maximum Credits: Total monthly credits cannot exceed 100% of monthly service fees
  • Exclusions: Credits do not apply during scheduled maintenance or customer-caused outages

8. Monitoring and Reporting

8.1 System Monitoring

  • 24/7 Monitoring: Automated monitoring of all infrastructure components
  • Real-time Alerts: Immediate notification of service issues
  • Performance Metrics: Continuous monitoring of CPU, memory, storage, and network
  • Status Page: Public status dashboard at status.datacaboose.com

8.2 Reporting

  • Monthly SLA Reports: Detailed uptime and performance reports
  • Quarterly Business Reviews: Service performance analysis and optimization recommendations
  • Incident Reports: Post-incident analysis and prevention measures
  • Capacity Planning: Proactive monitoring and scaling recommendations

9. Exclusions and Limitations

9.1 SLA Exclusions

This SLA does not apply to service interruptions caused by:

  • Scheduled maintenance performed within defined maintenance windows
  • Customer-initiated changes or misconfigurations
  • Third-party software issues outside DataCaboose control
  • Internet service provider issues affecting customer connectivity
  • Force majeure events (natural disasters, government actions, etc.)
  • DDoS attacks or other malicious activities targeting customer infrastructure
  • Customer violations of our Acceptable Use Policy

9.2 SLA Scope

  • SLA commitments apply only to DataCaboose-managed infrastructure
  • Customer applications and data are the customer's responsibility
  • Third-party integrations and external dependencies are excluded
  • Performance guarantees are based on baseline configurations
